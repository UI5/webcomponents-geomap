This sample showcases geofencing on a geo map
Features:
Geofence features are represented as geocircles, polygons, and lines on the map.
All can be edited directly on the map and support drag-and-drop.

Hover behavior:
Hovering over any feature highlights it by changing its color based on a configurable setting.

Selection behavior:
To drag-and-drop and edit, you need to click and select a feature.
