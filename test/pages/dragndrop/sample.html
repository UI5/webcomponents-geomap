This sample showcases interactive features on a geo map, including drag-and-drop and editing capabilities for various geometry types: spot, polygon, line, circle, and geocircle.
Editing support:
Polygons, lines, and geocircles can be edited directly on the map.
Spots and circles support drag-and-drop but do not support changes their size.

Hover behavior:
Hovering over any feature highlights it by changing its color based on a configurable setting.

Selection behavior:
To drag-and-drop and edit (where supported), you need to click and select a feature.
